About the conference

Three days. One mission: future-proof ideas.

Orbit isn’t another tech expo. It is a tightly curated gathering focused on what actually matters: AI systems people can trust, computing that scales sustainably, and open standards that outlast hype cycles.

Every session is chosen by peer review. Every talk is designed to be actionable — you leave with code, frameworks, or a concrete plan, not just swag.
